Overview

The main theme was "Pandora's Box". In legend she is said to have opened a box that released all sorts of evils into the world. The only thing that remained is hope. We decided to create a minature creature collector/turn-based combat system where you collect different personified evil entities that were released into the world. It is the player's goal to try and seal all the spirits back in the box.

Arcademia 2026

This project was created for the Arcademia 2026 game jam. Everything from planning to publishing was done in one weekend.

Our scope was overly ambitious, and we had to cut many things out. Despite that, I still think it is important to have this prototype in my portfolio. We were able to adapt to the situation and managed to submit something that plays. It also taught me a vital lesson about time management, and what is possible for me to accomplish in such a short window of time.
